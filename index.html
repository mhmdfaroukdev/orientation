BEFORE YOU ATTEND

A few useful answers.

The session is designed to remove uncertainty before onboarding and active club work begin.

Who should register?

Students invited to or interested in the Claude Builder Club @ AOU Egypt founding team orientation should register using their current contact details.

Will I receive the venue and final timing?

Yes. The email used during registration is the primary channel for confirmation and any schedule or venue updates.

Does registering mean final team selection?

No. Orientation registration records your attendance interest. Recruitment, interview and team-selection decisions remain separate.

Can I update my registration?

If you submitted incorrect information, contact the club team using the email below so the registration can be reviewed.
