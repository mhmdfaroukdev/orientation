ORIENTATION FAQ

Who should register?

Students invited or expected to attend the Claude Builder Club @ AOU Egypt founding-team orientation.

Do I need to register more than once?

No. One registration is enough. The system blocks duplicate registrations using the same email address.

When will the final date and room be announced?

Final event details will be sent to the email used during registration once confirmed.

What if I entered something incorrectly?

Contact the club team at claudebuilder.aou@gmail.com and include your registration ID if you have it.
