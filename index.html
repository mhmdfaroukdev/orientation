BEFORE YOU REGISTER

Quick answers.

Everything important about the registration process in one place.

Who should register?

Students invited to attend the Claude Builder Club @ AOU Egypt founding team orientation should complete this form.

Will I receive a confirmation?

Yes. After a successful registration, you will receive a unique registration ID and a confirmation email.

Can I submit the form twice?

No. The registration system prevents duplicate registrations using the same email address.

Where will the final time and venue be announced?

Final details and changes will be sent to the email address used during registration and may also be posted in the club updates channels.
